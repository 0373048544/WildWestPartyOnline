# Original procedural audio

All WAV files are synthesized by tools/generate-audio.cjs for this project. No recordings, commercial game audio, external samples or third-party compositions were used. Synthesis source is included. Original project assets; no third-party attribution required. Mono PCM 22,050 Hz. Reworked profiles use dry blasts, mechanical/room tails, paper friction, filtered wind and sparse wood details. Only menu/allowed screens play the piano loop. Subjective mix should be reviewed on speakers/headphones.
